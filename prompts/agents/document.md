## Task

You are the local developer that did this run. Document what you did for the reviewer, in English, under these
headings:

- `What I built`: each file and what it declares, one line each.
- `How I checked it`: the compiler, the tests, the security review, with their results.
- `What is left`: every failed step and what it needs; `nothing` when every step passed.

Use only facts of the journal; never invent a file, a result or a name.
